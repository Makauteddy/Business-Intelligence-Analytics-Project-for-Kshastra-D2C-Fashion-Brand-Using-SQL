# Business-Intelligence-Analytics-Project-for-Kshastra-D2C-Fashion-Brand-Using-SQL
This project analyzes the performance of kshastra, a Mumbai based direct to consumer fashion brand, using integrated datasets that cover Meta advertising campaigns, website activity, customer behavior, orders, order line items, inventory, and purchase orders. The purpose of the analysis is to understand how marketing efforts influence website traffic, customer acquisition, sales, inventory movement, and overall business performance. By examining data across the entire customer journey, from advertisement exposure to product purchase and returns, the project provides insights that can help improve marketing decisions, product planning, inventory management, customer retention, and profitability. The findings are intended to support data-driven decision-making as the company expands its revenue and strengthens its position in the Indian fashion e-commerce market.

# Problem Statement
Kshastra has experienced steady growth as a digital first fashion brand, but several business challenges are limiting its ability to scale efficiently. The company has a relatively low repeat purchase rate, high product return levels, dead stock accumulation, stock availability issues, and increasing logistics and material costs. Marketing and inventory decisions are still influenced heavily by intuition and limited analytical support, making it difficult to predict demand and allocate resources effectively. A comprehensive analysis of marketing, sales, customer, and inventory data is needed to identify the key factors affecting revenue, retention, operational efficiency, and profitability so that future business decisions can be made with greater confidence

# Project Objectives
1. Evaluate overall sales and revenue performance across products, categories, channels, cities, and time periods.
2. Measure the effectiveness of marketing campaigns by analyzing customer acquisition cost, return on ad spend, conversion rates, and campaign performance.
3. Understand customer purchasing behavior and improve customer retention by identifying repeat purchase patterns, customer lifetime value, and factors contributing to churn.
4. Optimize inventory and procurement decisions by examining stock levels, dead stock, stock outs, inventory turnover, and supplier lead times.
5. Reduce operational costs and improve profitability by analyzing returns, RTOs, discounts, shipping costs, and product level performance.

# Dataset Description

This project consists of interconnected datasets covering kshastra’s business operations from January 2022 to December 2025, including Meta Ads, Orders, Order Line Items, Customers, Website Users, Inventory, and Purchase Orders. The datasets capture the complete business journey from marketing campaigns and website activity to purchases, customer behavior, product returns, inventory levels, and supplier procurement. The project will use SQL to identify opportunities to increase revenue and customer retention, improve marketing efficiency, reduce returns and inventory risk, and support better business decision-making.
